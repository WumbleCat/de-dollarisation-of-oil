# PBoC RMB Internationalization Reports (tracker D44, secondary)

Discovered from http://www.pbc.gov.cn/en/3688241/3688636/3828468/index.html (year pages -> article pages -> PDF). Only the years that the
English site lists with a downloadable PDF are here (8 file(s) on 2026-10-21); older editions are
reachable from the Chinese site (人民币国际化报告) by hand. The reports are narrative PDFs - the clean
pipeline does not parse them. The monthly RMB-settlement series used for D44 comes from SAFE instead
(`raw/safe/crossborder_receipts_payments_timeseries.xls`, "By currency: RMB / Foreign Currency").
